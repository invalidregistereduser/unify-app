01About Unify

Unify is a student-focused mobile application created to improve how students of DII (dii.tj) access and interact with their university portal. Our goal is simple: take the existing portal.dii.tj web experience and turn it into a faster, cleaner, more comfortable mobile application that students can rely on every day.

Instead of opening a browser, logging in, waiting for pages to load, and hunting through tabs, Unify brings everything you need from the portal directly into one organized mobile app, with a modern academic design built for student life.

02What Unify offers

Unify is built as an improved mobile companion to portal.dii.tj. Features we provide or are building include:

Class notifications

Get notified before your next class starts, so you never miss a lecture, lab, or seminar.

GPA tracking

View your GPA and academic standing anytime, without digging through the portal.

Attendance monitoring

See which classes you've attended and know where you stand before it becomes a problem.

Schedule overview

Today's classes, tomorrow's classes, and your full weekly schedule at a glance.

Stay updated

Academic updates, announcements, and university notifications in a clean, organized feed.

Faster access

Everything from the portal, reorganized for mobile, with better speed and a better experience.

Our mission with Unify is to make student life at DII smoother, more organized, and more connected, without adding complexity.

03Data collection

We do not want to collect your personal data.

Unify is designed as a reader and presenter of information that already belongs to you and already exists on the official DII portal (portal.dii.tj).

The app works by connecting to the official DII portal using your own university login credentials. Any academic information, such as your schedule, GPA, attendance, or notifications, is retrieved from the university's own systems and shown to you inside the app. We do not store this information on separate servers for marketing or analytical purposes.

What we never do

  • Build profiles on students
  • Sell information
  • Share student data with advertisers
  • Run tracking or profiling for commercial purposes

The only data the app handles

  • Your university login, used only to sign you in to the DII portal, exactly as in a web browser.
  • Local device data such as notification preferences, saved on your device for class reminders.
  • Basic technical info such as crash reports, used only to fix bugs and improve stability.

We do not ask for data we don't need. We do not want data we don't need.
